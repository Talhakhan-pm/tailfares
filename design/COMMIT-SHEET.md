# COMMIT-SHEET — TailFares "The Journey"

## 1. Peak / Signature
Peak = Scene 3 "Night cargo": at ~50% scroll depth the white page floods to ink via letterbox; the electric plane crosses a Karachi→London great-circle arc on a dark route map, pinned 300vh, three fact captions sequenced to its progress. The page's only dark interior and only glow.

## 2. Color
Primary: oklch(0.55 0.20 262) — electric blue #2563EB. Tier: **restrained** — the page is ink-on-warm-white monochrome; electric appears ONLY on the journey line, plane, checkpoint stamps, links and CTAs. Not lavender (no gradient partner, single saturated hue with a semantic job); not cream-as-warmth-reflex — the base is #F7F7F5, a true near-chroma-zero off-white (oklch ≈ 0.975 0.002 106), warmth carried by type and copy, not tint. Category reflex (pet pastels + warm rounded friendliness) explicitly rejected: the audience's problem is trust, and the register that earns trust is editorial competence.
Background lightness: **L ≈ 0.97** page-wide; the peak scene interior inverts to **L ≈ 0.18** for its 300vh and returns. The page lives light because the brand's promise is transparency — a dark site would perform "premium logistics" (the second-order reflex) instead.

## 3. Type
Display: **Archivo** (grotesk with real width axis — condensed for departure-board headlines, aviation-signage voice). Text: **Manrope** (humanist-geometric, already licensed in the project, comfortable at 16–18px). Data: **IBM Plex Mono** for route codes, prices, timelines — the boarding-pass voice. Axis: signage grotesk × humanist text × mono data. Inter rejected as the 2024–26 AI default; Sora (current display) retired as near-reflex.

## 4. Grid break
The flight path is the grid break: a continuous SVG line that crosses every section boundary, and content cards in S2 sit alternately left/right of it (asymmetric split ~40/60 flipping per checkpoint). Plus the peak's full-bleed ink inversion interrupting the white page.

## 5. Motion budget
(1) **scroll-scrub** — the path drawing + checkpoint stamps (S2, S4); (2) **pinned-stage** — the night-cargo peak (S3 only); (3) **entrance-reveal** — split-flap headline + card/pass entrances (S1, S5, proof blocks). Nothing else scroll-triggered.

## 6. Reflex check
a) Generic AI pet-travel site: sky-blue + white, rounded cards, paw-print icons, golden-retriever hero photo, fade-up-on-scroll everywhere, "Stress-free pet travel!" copy.
b) Generic AI avoiding (a): dark "premium logistics" terminal — near-black, neon accent, mono everything, glow.
c) Ours: editorial ink-on-white monochrome where a single electric hue is reserved for the journey itself; the dark register appears only *inside* the film (one pinned night scene) as narrative, not as theme. Copy is concrete and slightly dry ("The part you're scared of is the part we've done 200 times."), no exclamation marks.

## 7. House tells broken
1. **Near-black background default** → warm-white L 0.97 page; ink exists only as the peak scene's interior, entered and exited on scroll.
2. **Scroll-instruction footer** ("scroll to explore") → none; the flight path visibly exits the hero fold, which *is* the scroll invitation.
3. **Amber-or-acid accent reflex** → electric blue #2563EB, chosen by the user, carrying a semantic job (the journey) rather than decoration.

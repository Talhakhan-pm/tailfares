# IPATA Membership — TailFares Application Plan

*Compiled Aug 14, 2026 from ipata.org/join-ipata and ipata.org/active-members
(application form updated April 2026). Fees verified on the Active Members page.*

## Why this matters

IPATA's "Find a Pet Shipper" directory is how retail customers AND foreign
agents (PetRelocation, Starwood, DKC, Airpets) find origin-side partners.
Pakistan has ~1 visible member. Provisional members get listed once
requirements are complete — that listing is the B2B flywheel.

## Costs and timeline

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Application fee (non-refundable) | $1,000 |
| Annual dues (2026) | $1,275 |
| IATA Live Animals Regulations (LAR) training, 1 staff member | $300 (developing-nations e-book rate, course CGC009EBEN01; exam 60 MCQ / 80% pass / 2 attempts; cert valid 36 months) |
| **Year-one total** | **~$2,575 (~PKR 720k)** |

Realistic path to submitted application: **2–4 months** (bottleneck below).
Then a 1-year provisional period: New Member Basics certification + exam
(80%+ required) before the public directory listing goes live.

## Requirements checklist

### Only you can do these
1. **Register the business in Pakistan** — legally registered at the operating
   location (sole proprietorship with NTN works to start; SECP Pvt Ltd looks
   better on the application). IPATA verifies this.
2. **Check Pakistan-side licensing** — IPATA requires international applicants
   to confirm transport-license rules with the Ministry of Agriculture (for
   Pakistan now NAFSA). Ask NAFSA in writing whether a commercial animal
   transport license/registration exists; keep the reply for the application.
3. **Insurance** — business liability policy copies "if required by country";
   even if Pakistan doesn't mandate it, having a policy strengthens a
   thin-history application.
4. **IATA LAR training** — one staff member (you) completes the IATA Live
   Animals Regulations course; certificate must carry the IATA logo or come
   from an authorized center. Online, self-paced.
5. **Pay and submit** — Active Member Application form (download from
   ipata.org/active-members), submitted with all documents by email.

### The strategic bottleneck: two reference letters
References must come from **Active IPATA pet-shipper members** (for the
provisional stage, both from pet shippers — airlines don't count) and must
attest that you delivered promised services, followed instructions,
prioritized pet welfare, and paid agreed fees.

A brand-new company has no IPATA relationships — so the references must be
EARNED through real jobs first. This inverts the usual order:

**Run 3–5 moves as the Pakistan-side partner of IPATA members BEFORE
applying.** Best targets (all IPATA members, all need Pakistan hands):
- **Snoopy Pets / DKC (Dubai)** — every PK→UAE move needs a Dubai-side agent
  anyway; use them as the destination handler, invoice cleanly, deliver
  flawlessly. UAE is route #1, so this costs nothing extra.
- **PetRelocation / Starwood (US)** — offer origin-side handling (vet runs,
  NAFSA paperwork, airport check-in) for their Pakistan bookings.

After a handful of clean jobs, the reference letters are a formality — and
those same relationships are the referral pipeline afterward.

### Prepped and waiting

- Company description for the application, drawn from the site: pet
  relocation from Pakistan (KHI/LHE/ISB) to UAE, UK, Canada, USA, Australia;
  documentation, crating, airline booking, and destination coordination.
